# Design system, navigation and UI design

The house design system is `D:\hoobi-design` (React and Tailwind, no XAML output); its `AGENTS.md` "WinUI 3 conventions" table is the spec this app ports into `Styles/Resources.axaml` by hand. Controls, cards, pills, alerts and flyouts carry no border, and fill alone tells surfaces apart; the only strokes are hairline dividers. Corners are 4px on controls and 8px on surfaces and overlays. Related cards under one heading form a group: a `StackPanel` with `Spacing="1"`, so the page ground shows through as the hairline, and the `GroupTopCornerRadius`, `GroupMiddleCornerRadius` and `GroupBottomCornerRadius` resources on its cards, so only the group's outer corners are rounded; on the Settings page a group is a `StackPanel` with `Classes="SettingsGroup"`, whose `Views/GroupEdges` behaviour marks the first and last visible card so a hidden card at either end leaves the outer corners rounded. A plain list of commands is an Avalonia `MenuFlyout` of `MenuItem`s, whose presenter gets the tinted look globally. A dropdown whose rows hold more than a menu item can (the account card, the account menu's guild submenu rows of icon, name, member count and role) is a custom `Flyout` with `FlyoutPresenterClasses="Tinted"` (add `Flush` for no padding), an explicit `Placement` and rows with menu item metrics. Both presenters take their tinted acrylic from `Styles/Acrylic` (hoobi-design's Mirage flyout blur with a darker tint: `FlyoutAcrylicTintColor` at `TintOpacity` 0.575 over a snapshot of the page behind, blurred at a sigma of 24 and saturated 1.4, the solid `FlyoutAcrylicFallbackColor` while the main window is not active; the full recipe is under Avalonia desktop app), so a view sets no `Background` on a presenter. A tooltip sets no `ToolTip.Placement`, since `Styles/PopupPlacement` places every one.

Every dialog subclasses `FAContentDialog` and is shown through `AppDialogs.ShowAsync(dialog, TopLevel.GetTopLevel(this)!)` (`src/Steward.Desktop/Services/AppDialogs.cs`), which applies the `Steward` class (tinted `ContentDialogBackground`, 8px corners, accent primary button, 440px width), closes any open flyout first and queues a dialog behind one already open; a dialog sets no brushes of its own, only a width override where its content needs one, and `Styles/Dialogs.axaml` supplies the scrim, the gap above the buttons and the text box and combo box look. A `PrimaryButtonClick` that does work takes `args.GetDeferral()`, as `MainWindow.ShowForeverCopyAsync` does. Two WinUI header metrics are set by hand: a `ComboBox` header has `LineHeight` 20, so its `FieldHeader` takes `Margin="0,0,0,10"`, and `RadioButtons` keeps an empty header with an 8px bottom margin and spaces its rows 8px, so its port is an `ItemsControl` with `Margin="0,8,0,0"` over a one-column `UniformGrid` with `RowSpacing="8"`.

A button with a clear rest background uses the `SubtleFillColor*` brushes for rest, hover and pressed. The Button template animates its background through a `BrushTransition` that interpolates ARGB, and `Transparent` is `#00FFFFFF`, so a clear rest brush against an opaque hover colour fades through white. A fade is safe when both ends share RGB (transparent to `SubtleFillColor*`, all white) or both are dark; a button with a clear rest background therefore uses `SubtleFillColorTransparentBrush` against `SubtleFillColor*`, or `ControlFillColorTransparentBrush` against an opaque fill, and a per-button override of hover or press colours goes in `Button.Styles` against the template part (`Selector="Button:pointerover /template/ ContentPresenter#PART_ContentPresenter"`). Button themes are set with `Theme="{StaticResource ...}"`: `DefaultButtonStyle`, `AccentButtonStyle`, `CriticalButtonStyle`, `CautionButtonStyle`, `SubtleButtonStyle` and `MenuRowButtonStyle` (a full-width subtle row for a flyout list). The segmented control (the Addons filter tabs, the Settings auto-update choice) is a `ListBox` with `Theme="{StaticResource SegmentedStyle}"` (`Styles/Controls.axaml`): equal-width items in a `UniformGrid` on `SegmentedTrackBrush` (`#19000000`) at 4px corners, items 29px high at 13px with 11px side padding, hover and press on an inset `PART_Hover` with the subtle fills, and the selected item on `ControlFillColorDefaultBrush` with a 16x3 accent pill at the bottom; an item selects on release over it, not on press (`Styles/SegmentedSelection`, switched on by the theme). Its items fade from `ControlFillColorTransparentBrush` (`#001A1F2B`) to the opaque `ControlFillColorDefaultBrush` on selection, so the fade never passes through white. A `TextBlock` with `Classes="FieldHeader"` (13px, `TextControlHeaderForeground`, 8px below) sits above a `TextBox` or `ComboBox` in place of a `Header`, `Classes="FieldHeader ComboBox"` for a `ComboBox`; a `FontSize` is set when the control sets its own.

Text inside a filled box (a read-only value field, pill, chip, tile or button) is vertically centred in it: the box takes a fixed height (32px, `TextControlThemeMinHeight`, for anything sitting beside a `TextBox` or `Button`) and the `TextBlock` inside sets `VerticalAlignment="Center"`, never top-aligned text with padding standing in for centring. A read-only value shown where an editable one would be is a `TextBox` with `IsReadOnly="True"`, so it gets the same metrics as its neighbours. Checked in a screenshot, the ink's gap above and below is equal within 1px; top-aligned text in a box has been built and flagged more than once. Text in two fonts on one line (a mono version beside proportional text) is one `TextBlock` of `Run`s, so every part sits on one baseline; separate `TextBlock`s centre by box height and the mono part rides high.

Every top-level page (`HomePage`, `SyncPage`, `GuidesPage`, `SettingsPage`) is a DI singleton registered in `App.axaml.cs`, constructor-injected with `MainViewModel`, and the shell shows one by assigning it to `PageHost.Content` in `MainWindow.ShowPage`, chosen by the nav item's `Tag` in `MainWindow.Navigate`, never by building a new page per visit. In the WinUI build a new page per visit left each discarded page's bound repeaters and lists subscribed to the shared `MainViewModel` collections, and on 28 Sep 2026 one of those threw a bare `COMException 0x80004005` from a `TableRows` removal after a few Settings round trips. Pages get no navigation callbacks, so a page reacts to its view model or to its own `Loaded`/`Unloaded` (the Settings page's pending Edit install request), and `ShowPage` plays the entrance animation itself. A new page is registered as a singleton and added to the tag switch in `MainWindow.Navigate`. The Sync item shows only while the selected install is a Forever client (`MainViewModel.IsForeverSelected`); when the Sync or Guides item hides while its page shows, `MainWindow.OnViewModelPropertyChanged` navigates to Addons (it does nothing once `MainViewModel.IsDisposed`, since disposal clears the installs after the container that resolves pages is gone), and an expired RestedXP session keeps the Guides page open. The pattern and its tradeoffs are in `D:\earmark\docs\adr\navigation-singleton-pages.md`.

The window extends content into the title bar (see Avalonia desktop app for the drawn caption buttons). The title bar holds only the install picker (`InstallPicker`), in `InstallPickerHost`, which spans the column between the title and the caption buttons. `MainWindow.PositionInstallPicker` runs on every title bar `LayoutUpdated` and sets the picker's `InstallPickerOffset.X` so it is centred on the whole window, clamped to 8px from the title on the left and from the caption buttons' drag gutter on the right, so a narrow window slides it beside whichever it would overlap; when the column is narrower than the picker plus both gaps it sits right-aligned in the column. The picker is at least 32px high (40px with the countdown line, 4px lower) and vertically centred in the 48px (`TitleBarHeight`) title bar. The countdown and the schedule follow the selected install's product (`AppProducts.Schedule`): the events of that product's steward-server entry when it carries a non-null `events` list (`[]` means no timers), else for `wowf` and `wow_classic_beta` the bundled `ForeverSchedule.Events`, else none (see Products). Under the install label the picker reads the time to the headline event (`Headline`, the launch in the bundled list) as "{n days m hours}" in 11px secondary text (`LaunchCountdownText`, `ForeverSchedule.TitleCountdown`, refreshed every 30 seconds, then "Live now" until the event's end, or for `ForeverSchedule.LiveFor` (24 hours) after its start when it has none, then gone; with no headline there is no countdown; while it shows, the picker's `Countdown` class makes its icon 24px and gives it a 4px top margin, and without it the picker is one line at 32px with a 20px icon), and the bottom of its flyout lists each event still ahead under the product's display name (`ScheduleTitle`): in the bundled list the stress test, name reservations and the launch at Blizzard's Americas and Oceania times (news.blizzard.com, read 21 Oct 2026), in local time with the event's `UpcomingText` before its start and `ActiveText` between its start and end, `{time}` replaced with the humanised span (defaults "Starts in {time}" and "Ends in {time}"; the bundled name reservations read "Live now" while open); an entry with no end (the stress test) leaves the list once it starts, and name reservations, given only as ending on 3 Nov, end at 23:59 Pacific that day. The 30-second timer runs only while a countdown or a row shows, and `UpdateCountdown` starts or stops it on every product fetch and every `SelectedInstall` change. Launch gating (`ForeverLaunch.HasNoCharacters`, `ReadsCharacters` and the journey upload loop) reads the compiled `ForeverSchedule.Launch` only, never server data. The picker is collapsed while signed out, and shows on Settings too so the countdown is on every page. Its "Manage installs" item opens Settings and brings the Installs card into view (`SettingsPage.ShowInstalls`), since the singleton Settings page keeps its scroll offset between visits. The install flyout (260px) opens centred below the picker (`Placement="Bottom"`) and passes its dismissing press through to `InstallPicker` (`OverlayInputPassThroughElement`).

The account entry (`AccountButton`, the Discord avatar and name) is a plain `Button` in the pane's footer, directly above the Settings item, rather than a nav item, so opening it never changes the selected page. It takes the nav items' metrics (36px high, 4px inset and 4px from the Settings item, 4px corners, a 40px icon column holding the 24px avatar, the `NavigationViewItemBackground*` brushes), shows the avatar alone in the compact pane with the user name and live-updates state as its tooltip and automation name (`MainViewModel.AccountAutomationName`, only the parts that are set joined with ", ", so it reads the live-updates state alone before the user name loads), and is visible only while signed in, with the rest of the pane. `AccountFlyout` opens to its right with its bottom edge 8px above the window's (`MainWindow.PlaceAccountFlyout`, a `VerticalOffset` set in `Opening`), and the guild submenu keeps opening to the right of its row, top-aligned. The role pill sits right-aligned on the name line when it fits beside the name with an 8px gap, else below the handle (`MainWindow.PlaceRolePill`, on either one's `SizeChanged`).

`docs/design/home-and-settings.md` is the agreed design for the settings page and the shell, and it is implemented. Four things in it were behaviour rather than view, and the doc remains the source of truth for each: the release channel is per addon (`AppState.Channels`, with `LegacyChannel` carrying the one-time migration), refresh probes every channel per addon (`release`, `pre-release`) so a channel with no release can be disabled, the default channel is the first one that has a release in that order, and the timer refreshes manifests as well as the role. Applying an update follows the game client. It no longer describes the Addons page or the Release channels card; `addon-manager.md` covers both.

`docs/design/addon-manager.md` is the agreed redesign of the Addons page into a general addon manager: one table for a selected install, an install picker with user labels and a required game version, release channels edited in a dialog from the row instead of on Settings, per-install Ignore, Local rows for unmanaged folders and an out of date TOC flag. Phase 1 is implemented and that doc is the source of truth for the Addons page; phase 2's Get addons dialog and CurseForge source are built (see Managed addons, and `curseforge.md` for everything CurseForge), and Wago is not. The install picker lives in the title bar, centred on the window and slid aside when that would overlap the title or caption buttons (see Navigation; hidden while signed out), and its one app-wide selection (`MainViewModel.SelectedInstall`, `selected_install`) scopes what the Addons, Sync and Guides pages show: `SyncViewModel.Installs` holds only the selected install, whose datasets and the "Guild data in game" row form one card with no install heading, and the Guides page shows `MainViewModel.SelectedGuide` as one grouped card (its product rows, then a `Guides.lua` row carrying Write again) under a single "{username} · {BattleTag} · checked {relative}" line with Sign out, or a "RestedXP Guides is not installed on {install}." card. Background work still covers every install. While an install of a client other than Forever is selected, the Sync nav item is hidden (`MainViewModel.IsForeverSelected` in `SyncVisibility`), and when that selection is made while the Sync page shows, `MainWindow` navigates to Addons. Guides works on every client.

`docs/design/guides-page.md` is the agreed design for the Guides page and the RestedXP sign-in dialog, and both are built against it; the doc stays the source of truth. The row control is a `CheckBox` and any number of owned products are kept per install, each checked row carrying its own pill; a product outside the install's client gate renders disabled and unchecked with a dim "Not for this client" line in place of the updated time. There is no "Waiting for game to close" state, since the generated addon is read at login or `/reload`. The `Guides` menu item shows only while RXPGuides is installed in some WoW install and a RestedXP session exists, and the sign-in entry point is a button on the RestedXP row of the Addons page; the page navigates back to Addons when either condition drops. The ninth state, `Needs a newer guide`, is rendered from `GuideSyncOutcome.NeedsNewerAddon`, which nothing sets yet: the service does not compare the written string's minimum addon version against the installed RXPGuides, so that state is reachable only through the preview. The product list (`/user-products` and the timestamps) is held in memory only, so until the first fetch of a session completes the card shows a "Loading your guides from RestedXP…" row with a `ProgressRing` (`RestedXpInstallViewModel.LoadingVisibility`, from `RestedXpViewModel.IsCatalogueLoading`: signed in, no catalogue yet (`RestedXpService.HasCatalogue`, cleared on sign-out), and no failure since that fetch started). A later Refresh or 3-hour check keeps the loaded rows in place with no loading row, a failed first fetch shows neither row beside its error, and "No guides on this account" shows only once a fetch has completed with no products (`IsCatalogueLoaded`). `--guides-preview=<scenario>` (`src/Steward.Presentation/Services/GuidesPreview.cs`) seeds the view models with a fake session and one of the page or dialog states, calls no network and writes no files, and is how the states are screenshotted without an account.

`docs/design/sync.md` is the original design for the Sync page, and the page and the shell it describes are built; `character-sync.md` and `roster-sync.md` are the source of truth for what the page syncs. Loot and attendance have no rows and no endpoints yet.
